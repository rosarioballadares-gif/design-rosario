# Brief: a Stop hook sends a prose question back to the question UI

## Objective

A turn that ends on a question in prose, or on a brief, with no
`AskUserQuestion` call, is stopped once and sent back to ask through the UI.

> SOURCE: Ian Rueff, 2026-10-09 — "un hook mejor q claude md para forzar el
> uso de ask user question porq noto q aveces no se triggerea"

## Differentiator

Today the rule is prose in [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md), "How to ask" and the
gate's step 2, and prose does not fire: this session closed two turns on prose
questions with the rule in context.

> SOURCE: the agent, 2026-10-09, this session's transcript

## Context and rules

- `Stop` is the one event at the moment the rule breaks. It reads the turn
  from `transcript_path`; `{"decision":"block","reason"}` makes the agent
  continue.
- It reads what follows the last tool call, and fires when its last
  paragraph outside code and quotes carries `?` or `¿`, or it carries four or
  more of the brief's field headings. An earlier `AskUserQuestion` does not
  excuse it: the replay missed this session's push turn until it stopped.
- `stop_hook_active` passes: one nudge, never a loop. It fails open, like the
  other guards.

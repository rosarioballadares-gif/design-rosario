# PDR-NNNN — <the decision, as a sentence somebody could disagree with>

## Context

What was true before, measured rather than remembered. The constraint, the
symptom, or the thing that stopped working. If a number appears here, say where
it came from and when it was read.

This section is where a decision goes wrong quietly: a plausible sentence about
the current state reads exactly like a measured one, and everything downstream
inherits it.

## Decision

What was chosen, in the present tense, as a rule somebody can follow. Not "we
should" — what is now the case.

## Rationale

Why this and not the alternatives. Name at least one option that was ruled out
and what ruled it out, because a record with no discarded option is a summary
rather than a decision.

## Consequences

What this costs, what it now makes impossible, and what somebody has to do
differently. The costs are the half that gets skipped, and they are the half a
future reader needs — the benefits are already in the Rationale.

## Related

Records this supersedes or descends from, the brief it was executed under, and
anything outside this repository that has to be named in full.
